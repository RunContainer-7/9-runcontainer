## Try these first

1. **Close everything and try again.** Close the application, wait for its environment to stop, then retry.
2. **Restart your PC.** This clears many temporary problems.
3. **Try a fresh environment.** If an application works in a new environment, the original environment may be in a bad state.
4. **Check the [Known limitations](/reference/limitations).** The behaviour may be expected.

## Still stuck?

Get help on the [Support page](/support), which links to the RunContainer Discord server. To report a bug, see [Contributing → Reporting bugs](/reference/contributing#reporting-bugs) for what to include.

::: warning Share safely
Before sharing screenshots or error messages, remove usernames, file paths containing your name, licence keys and anything else personal.
:::
